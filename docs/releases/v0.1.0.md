## Bloodborne for Mac 0.1.0

The first release of this project. It is based on
[bloodborne_mac_port](https://github.com/Wooloomooloo2/bloodborne_mac_port) 0.5.15 (Rosetta 2
runtime, native Metal renderer, launcher) and incorporates code from
[bloodborne-mac](https://github.com/bloodborne-native/bloodborne-mac).

### What's new compared with bloodborne_mac_port

- **Dark Souls III-style keyboard and mouse.** E interacts, Q locks on, Space taps to dodge and
  holds to run, F transforms, V drinks a Blood Vial, Shift + wheel switches weapons and the
  wheel cycles quick items. Every key can be changed on the launcher's **Controls** page.
- **The mouse works in the game's menus**, and the key hints show your keys or your pad's
  buttons, whichever you used last.
- **Menus no longer leak into play:** closing a list with Q, Space or Esc while holding
  W/A/S/D does not switch weapons, items or blood bullets.
- **The launcher speaks English, 日本語 and 简体中文.**
- **A faster start:** a checkout builds only when a source changed, and the prepared game image
  is kept between starts.
- **Display:** native 3840 × 1620 (21:9) and MetalFX Spatial as an option.
- **Two editions:** single-player, and multiplayer with online play (messages, bloodstains,
  bells and summons through The Hunter's Dream or shadNet) on the launcher's new **Online** tab.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/Sqhh99/Bloodborne-for-Mac/blob/master/docs/CHANGELOG.md).

### Known limits

- Online play has been tested by upstream with its own builds. This project's multiplayer
  build has passed its unit tests but has not yet been tested in a co-op session.
- Upstream's [known issues](https://github.com/Sqhh99/Bloodborne-for-Mac#known-issues) apply.

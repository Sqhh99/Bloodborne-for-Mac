## Bloodborne for Mac 0.1.3

- **One download per release:** `Bloodborne-macOS-0.1.3.zip`. The PKG unpacker that used to be
  a separate `pkg_extract-macOS.zip` is now inside the app, at
  `Bloodborne.app/Contents/Resources/tools/pkg_extract`. See the README's usage guide
  (docs/USAGE.md) for how to prepare the game folder with it.

**Updating:** from 0.1.2 the app finds this update by itself. From 0.1.0 or 0.1.1, download
the zip once and replace `Bloodborne.app`; your saves and settings stay where they are.

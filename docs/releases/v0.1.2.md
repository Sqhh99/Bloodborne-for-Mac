## Bloodborne for Mac 0.1.2

- **The app takes the game's icon.** After you choose your game folder, Bloodborne.app shows
  your game's own icon in Finder, Launchpad and the Dock (the download itself carries only the
  app's own icon: no game artwork is distributed).
- **The Dock icon stays the same** when the app opens, when the game starts and when it ends
  (from the second launch, once the app has taken the game's icon).
- **The launcher hides while you play** and comes back when the game ends. Turn **Hide
  launcher while playing** off under General to keep it on screen.
- **One download.** There is no separate single-player app any more: `Bloodborne-macOS-0.1.2.zip`
  has online play built in, off until you turn it on in the **Online** tab.

**Updating:** 0.1.1's multiplayer app finds this update by itself. From 0.1.0, or from
0.1.1's single-player app, download the zip once and replace `Bloodborne.app`; your saves and
settings stay where they are.

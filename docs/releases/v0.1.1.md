## Bloodborne for Mac 0.1.1

- **Icons fixed.** The app showed a blank icon in Finder and in the Dock, and so did the game
  window while playing. The app now has its own icon. Once the launcher has checked your game
  folder, both the launcher and the game window show the game's artwork in the Dock.
- **One release per version.** Both editions are now downloads of the same release; choose the
  single-player or the multiplayer zip.

**Coming from 0.1.0:** 0.1.0's update check cannot find this release. Download your edition's
zip once and replace `Bloodborne.app`; your saves and settings stay where they are. Later
updates are found automatically again.

## Bloodborne for Mac 0.1.4

- **Two icons, kept apart.** The launcher always shows Bloodborne for Mac's own icon, in
  Finder, Launchpad, the Dock and window thumbnails. The game window shows your game's icon.
  0.1.2 and 0.1.3 gave the launcher the game's icon; that is undone the first time 0.1.4 starts.
- **A refined app icon:** a pale moon over a misty city of spires.

From 0.1.2 or 0.1.3 the app finds this update by itself. macOS may keep showing the old icon
in the Dock or Finder for a moment after the update.
